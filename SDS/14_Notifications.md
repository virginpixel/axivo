# Axivo Software Design Specification

# Document 14 - Notifications

## Chapter 1 - Notifications Module Overview

### Purpose

The Notifications module manages all emails, in-app notifications and future messaging channels generated by Axivo.

It delivers approval requests, reminders, acknowledgements, credential notifications and system alerts while maintaining a complete delivery history.

---

### Objectives

- Centralize notification management
- Support multiple notification channels
- Track delivery status
- Support templates and personalization
- Maintain notification history
- Integrate with all business modules

---

### Module Scope

The module manages:

- Notification Templates
- Email Notifications
- In-App Notifications
- Reminder Scheduling
- Delivery History
- Notification Preferences

---

### Relationships

Notifications integrate with:

- Requests
- Workflows
- People
- Documents
- Applications
- Assets
- Audit

---

### Design Principles

- Notifications are event-driven.
- Delivery history is immutable.
- Templates are reusable.
- Every notification event is audited.

End of Chapter 1.


---

## Chapter 2 - Notification Templates

### Purpose

Notification Templates define reusable message formats for all system-generated notifications.

Templates support consistent communication while allowing dynamic content insertion.

---

### Supported Template Types

The system includes templates for:

- Approval Required
- Approval Completed
- Request Rejected
- Correction Requested
- Credential Delivery
- Asset Handover
- Clearance
- Reminder
- System Alert

Administrators may create additional templates.

---

### Template Fields

Each template contains:

- Template Name
- Notification Type
- Subject
- Message Body
- Supported Variables
- Active Status

---

### Dynamic Variables

Templates may include variables such as:

- Employee Name
- Request Number
- Workflow Step
- Company Name
- Asset Tag
- Approval Link
- Expiry Date

Variables are resolved automatically at send time.

---

### Business Rules

- Templates are versioned.
- Disabled templates cannot be used for new notifications.
- Existing delivery history remains unchanged.

---

### Audit

Audit events:

- Template created
- Template updated
- Template enabled
- Template disabled

End of Chapter 2.

## Chapter 3 - Notification Channels

### Purpose

This chapter defines the communication channels supported by the Notifications module.

---

### Supported Channels

The system supports:

- Email
- In-App Notifications
- Future SMS
- Future Push Notifications
- Future Microsoft Teams
- Future Slack

Channels may be enabled or disabled by administrators.

---

### Channel Configuration

Each channel defines:

- Channel Name
- Active Status
- Priority
- Retry Policy
- Delivery Timeout

---

### Delivery Rules

- Email is the default channel for workflow approvals.
- In-app notifications complement email where users have portal access.
- Multiple channels may be used for a single event.
- Failed delivery on one channel does not prevent delivery on others.

---

### Business Rules

- Notification channels respect user permissions and company boundaries.
- Channel availability is configurable.
- Delivery attempts are recorded.

---

### Audit

Audit events:

- Channel enabled
- Channel disabled
- Delivery attempted
- Delivery succeeded
- Delivery failed

End of Chapter 3.

## Chapter 4 - Notification Events

### Purpose

This chapter defines the business events that generate notifications within Axivo.

---

### Standard Notification Events

Notifications are generated for:

- Request Submitted
- Approval Required
- Approval Approved
- Approval Rejected
- Correction Requested
- Correction Submitted
- Implementation Required
- Credential Delivery
- Asset Handover
- Clearance Completion
- License Expiry Reminder
- Contract Renewal Reminder
- System Alert

Additional events may be configured by administrators.

---

### Event Processing

For each event the system shall:

1. Resolve recipients.
2. Select the appropriate template.
3. Resolve template variables.
4. Select notification channels.
5. Queue the notification for delivery.

---

### Business Rules

- Notifications are generated only after successful completion of the triggering event.
- Duplicate notifications for the same event shall be prevented unless explicitly configured.
- Failed deliveries follow the configured retry policy.

---

### Audit

Audit events:

- Notification queued
- Notification generated
- Duplicate prevented
- Event processed

End of Chapter 4.

## Chapter 5 - Recipient Resolution

### Purpose

This chapter defines how notification recipients are identified before delivery.

---

### Recipient Sources

Recipients may be resolved from:

- Requester
- Requested For
- Workflow Approver
- Delegated Approver
- IT Implementation User
- Department Head
- HR
- General Manager
- Configured Administrative Recipients

---

### Resolution Process

For every notification the system shall:

1. Identify the notification event.
2. Resolve all eligible recipients.
3. Remove duplicate recipients.
4. Verify recipient permissions.
5. Queue the notification.

---

### Business Rules

- Notifications are delivered only to authorized recipients.
- Company boundaries shall always be respected.
- Disabled users do not receive notifications.
- Delegated approvers receive notifications only during the active delegation period.

---

### Audit

Audit events:

- Recipient resolved
- Recipient skipped
- Duplicate recipient removed
- Notification queued

End of Chapter 5.

## Chapter 6 - Reminder Scheduling & Delivery

### Purpose

This chapter defines reminder scheduling, retry behavior and delivery management for notifications.

---

### Reminder Types

The system supports reminders for:

- Pending Approvals
- Pending Corrections
- Pending IT Implementations
- License Renewals
- Contract Renewals
- Asset Acknowledgements
- Clearance Tasks

Reminder schedules are configurable by administrators.

---

### Retry Policy

If delivery fails:

- The notification is retried according to the configured retry policy.
- Retry intervals are configurable.
- Permanent failures are recorded.
- Administrators may manually resend notifications.

---

### Delivery Status

Each notification records one of the following statuses:

- Queued
- Sending
- Delivered
- Failed
- Cancelled
- Expired

Historical delivery records remain immutable.

---

### Business Rules

- Reminder generation shall not create duplicate active reminders for the same event unless explicitly configured.
- Expired notifications are not resent automatically.
- Manual resend actions create a new delivery record while preserving the original.

---

### Audit

Audit events:

- Reminder scheduled
- Reminder sent
- Retry attempted
- Manual resend
- Delivery expired

End of Chapter 6.

## Chapter 7 - Notification Preferences & User Controls

### Purpose

This chapter defines how notification preferences are configured while ensuring mandatory business notifications are always delivered.

---

### User Preferences

Users may configure preferences for:

- In-App Notifications
- Informational Emails
- Reminder Frequency
- Notification Digest (future)

Mandatory workflow notifications cannot be disabled.

---

### Administrative Controls

Administrators may configure:

- Default notification channels
- Reminder schedules
- Retry policies
- Sender identities
- Company-specific branding

---

### Business Rules

- Approval requests are always delivered regardless of user preferences.
- Security notifications cannot be disabled.
- Company settings override user preferences where required.
- Preference changes affect only future notifications.

---

### Preference Inheritance

Notification settings are applied in the following order:

1. System defaults
2. Company settings
3. User preferences

Mandatory notifications always take precedence.

---

### Audit

Audit events:

- Preference updated
- Company notification settings changed
- Mandatory notification delivered
- Preference override applied

End of Chapter 7.

## Chapter 10 - Validation, Permissions & Security

### Purpose

This chapter defines the validation rules, permission model and security controls for the Notifications module.

---

### Validation Rules

Before any notification is queued or delivered, the system shall validate:

- Valid notification event
- Active template
- Valid recipients
- Company ownership
- Supported delivery channel
- Required template variables
- Delivery configuration

Server-side validation is mandatory.

---

### Permissions

System Administrator:
- Full access

IT Administrator:
- Notification management where permitted

Authorized Users:
- View only notifications related to records they are permitted to access

Read Only:
- View notification history where permitted

---

### Security

Every notification operation shall:

- Verify authentication
- Verify authorization
- Validate all input
- Protect sensitive data contained in notifications
- Record an immutable audit event

Notification history shall never be modified after delivery.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

- Unauthorized access is prevented.
- Invalid notifications are rejected.
- Delivery history remains immutable.
- Audit history is complete.

End of Chapter 10.
